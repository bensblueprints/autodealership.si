# DealerOn feature gaps

Comparison of public DealerOn US product descriptions and the AutoDealership.SI implementation reviewed November 27, 2026. OEM enrollment and paid add-ons vary by program. A sales page or example campaign on our site does not establish a working provider integration.

| What a dealer can buy from DealerOn | What AutoDealership.SI has | What remains to deliver |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Listings and reputation management across multiple directories, public reviews and social accounts | Store hours, contact data, a dealer-selected Google review link, an attended-visit/purchase queue, permission-based post-purchase Resend emails and private feedback follow-up | Authorized public review sync and replies, SMS invitations, listings distribution, conflict detection and per-location reporting. [Listings Manager](https://www.dealeron.com/listings-manager/) |
| Digital retail with credit decisions, trade valuation, incentive selection, F&I products and document uploads | Per-car cash and BHPH schedules, illustrative financing, appraisal requests, appointments, staff-recorded contracts and values | Licensed valuation, lender decisions and a saved online deal that a finance manager can finish. A staff-recorded purchase is a sales record. E-signing and funding require separate workflows. [Apex Digital Retail](https://www.dealeron.com/apex-digital-retail/) |
| VIN-specific incentive displays, payments and specials automation | Prices, window sticker data where available, dealer-authored offers, dated approvals and an inventory ad feed | Licensed rebate data, eligibility by VIN, region and buyer, stacking rules, lender/lease terms and publishing approved specials across channels. [DealerOn incentives](https://www.dealeron.com/features-and-upgrades/offers-and-incentives/) |
| Advertising programs and performance reporting | Google vehicle feed, campaign review, operational conversion exports and deduplicated online bookings | Live channel delivery and spend reconciliation, call and ad attribution down to VIN and a sale matched to the dealer’s records. A signed amount adds an outcome without establishing advertising attribution. [Acura program](https://www.dealeron.com/acura/) · [Product updates](https://www.dealeron.com/new-product-updates/) |
| Published OEM enrollment programs and ongoing compliance monitoring | Franchise templates, a private brand library, page and offer approvals, version history, and daily or staff-initiated reports on storefront placement and source validity | Written acceptance per OEM, official program data and tags, rendered-page checks against each OEM’s rules, screenshot evidence and co-op submission. [OEM programs](https://www.dealeron.com/oem-programs/) · [OnGuard updates](https://www.dealeron.com/new-product-updates/) |
| AI-search visibility tracking, site personalization, a scheduled blog and merchandising tools such as narrated 360° spins | Crawlable inventory, editable pages, photo galleries, related vehicles, stock-aware voice concierge and booking | Prompt/rank and competitor monitoring, consent-aware behavioral offers, a full article scheduler, 360 media and per-VIN performance diagnostics. Some DealerOn AI offerings are described as beta. [DealerOn product updates](https://www.dealeron.com/new-product-updates/) |

**Build order for independents.** Finish review invitations and the Google inbox; connect one real CRM or DMS and reconcile sales; then demonstrate cost per booked visit, attended visit and purchase. Track first-party inquiry, attendance and purchase separately. Price optional integrations against the dealer’s volume.

**Build order for franchises.** Start OEM program intake and identify a pilot rooftop while building the independent product. Prioritize feed monitoring and licensed incentives once a dealer supplies the actual source systems. Add compliance monitoring and evidence for the requesting manufacturer before expanding to another program.
