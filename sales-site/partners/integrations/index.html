Inside the platform

Select a screenshot to inspect the controls at full size. Screens use demonstration inventory and shopper records.

Start an integration conversation with the record that must arrive

An integration request becomes testable when the dealership can name the record, its owner and its destination. A vehicle feed, a customer inquiry, an appointment, an email reply and a sold outcome each carry different permissions and identifiers.

Check the existing connection

The platform uses NHTSA vPIC for available VIN data, Clerk for staff identity, ElevenLabs for voice plans and Whop for subscriptions. Dealer-owned Resend teams can grant email access through OAuth. Each connection has its own purpose and activation steps.

Specify a dealer system request

Give the exact CRM or DMS name, dealer code, provider contact and direction of transfer. Ask which party is authorized to send a lead or read inventory. Supply a sample record with customer information removed.

Review changes and failures

Decide what should happen to a changed stock number, a duplicate inquiry and a sold vehicle. The receiving system should identify the same location and event after a retry. Verify delivery using the recipient’s actual acknowledgment.

Questions dealers ask

Does being listed here imply that every dealer can activate a CRM or DMS?

Availability depends on the provider, product, permissions and a tested connection for the rooftop. Ask about your exact vendor and workflow during discovery.

Who controls our email provider relationship?

With dealer-owned Resend OAuth, the dealership selects the team and controls its provider relationship. Confirm the sending domain and authorized workflows for the specific store.

Bring one redacted example

Choose a lead or inventory record from the two systems you want to connect. Identify which fields must arrive, what proves delivery and who handles exceptions.
