Dealer solutions

Dealer groups

Give each rooftop its own inventory, staff access and branded website while the platform team manages setup.

Book a walkthrough ↗View platform screens ↓

Inside the platform

Select a screenshot to inspect the controls at full size. Screens use demonstration inventory and shopper records.

Give each rooftop a clear owner

A group website project can look complete while leads travel to the wrong location. Establish the store identity, staff access, inventory and handoff at one rooftop before using that configuration as a model elsewhere.

Create the first preview

Choose a distinctive subdomain and the correct dealership name. Assign the engineer, identify the store administrator and review branding, department lines and the time zone.

Test separation from a second location

Use two inventory samples and two staff accounts during testing. A salesperson should be able to find their own store’s customer and vehicle while every shopper sees the correct public address and number.

Sequence the remaining sites

Decide which settings belong to the group and which each general manager owns. Record the next store’s DNS owner, training time and acceptance contact before scheduling its cutover.

Questions dealers ask

Can our engineers prepare a store before it pays?

A platform administrator or assigned engineer can prepare a client preview and its dealership configuration before the commercial activation step.

Can group leaders see all sales attributed across rooftops?

Cross-rooftop revenue attribution is on the roadmap. Define which dealership system owns sold outcomes before setting group reporting expectations.

Check a same-name model at two lots

Use different stock numbers for similar vehicles at two locations. Follow a shopper question through to the receiving store and verify the second store’s inventory stays separate.
