Buy Here Pay Here dealers

Publish the cash price and in-house financing schedule the dealership actually offers.

Explore the showroom ↗View platform screens ↓

Inside the platform

Select a screenshot to inspect the controls at full size. Screens use demonstration inventory and shopper records.

Sell the visit with a schedule the shopper can read

A customer paid every other Friday may ask about cash due today before asking about the vehicle’s trim. The store needs to show the actual schedule it offers and give its salesperson enough context to answer the next question.

Build one real offer

Use the price your store charges for cash, the BHPH price, the required cash down and the balance your staff expects to finance. Select the offered payment cadence and term, then read the full schedule before publishing.

Check exceptions by vehicle

A cash-only trade or a vehicle awaiting finance terms can have a different pricing setup. Review how each appears when conventional APR estimates are switched off site-wide.

Prepare the salesperson

Let the shopper identify a stock number and ask whether their trade or payday can work. The dealership evaluates the trade and final terms; the staff record preserves what the shopper wants to discuss.

Questions dealers ask

What if everything is already included in our price?

Start from the amount the store intends to display and check it against the calculated balance and schedule. The dealer should review any separately configured fees so it can explain the final figures.

Does this handle collections after delivery?

The showroom supports pre-sale affordability, shopper questions and appointments. Post-sale servicing, collections and payoff accounting require a servicing system.

Bring a paper payout schedule

Choose one real stock number and cover personal information. Compare the dealer’s schedule with the public cash-down amount, payment frequency, term and final installment.
