Dealer sign-in

Open the private dealer dashboard for your AutoDealership.SI showroom.

.autodealership.si
Platform admin ↗Engineer sign-in ↗

Start with the dealership where you work

Your dealership website and the platform administration area serve different jobs. Inventory, shopper inquiries and scheduled test drives belong in the private dashboard for the specific rooftop that invited you.

Find the right sign-in

Use the exact subdomain provided in the store’s welcome link, or open Dealer sign-in in that showroom’s footer. On the sign-in screen, use the work email the store administrator invited.

Check which store opened

Confirm the dealership name before editing a vehicle. A group employee may have access to more than one store, and the stock number or shopper name alone may match a record elsewhere.

Recover an invitation

If access is missing after sign-in, ask the dealership administrator to check the invited address, organization and membership. Include the subdomain and time of the attempt when contacting support.

Questions dealers ask

Why does my billing account open a different screen?

The billing account manages subscriptions. Dealer staff access inventory and leads through their showroom’s private dealer dashboard.

Where do owners and engineers manage client sites?

Use the platform administrator or engineer sign-in links on this page. These workspaces use the permissions assigned to your platform identity.

Confirm a new employee’s access

Have the employee open the showroom footer themselves, sign in and identify the dealership name and the inventory or inquiry tab they need.
