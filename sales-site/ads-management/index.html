Inside the platform

Select a screenshot to inspect the controls at full size. Screens use demonstration inventory and shopper records.

Choose a campaign around the shopper’s next decision

Campaign Studio gives a dealership a way to review the creative, offer and destination together. A shopper exploring options needs a different ad from one ready to ask about a specific stock number.

Start with an audience problem

A trade-in shopper wants to understand what their current vehicle might contribute. A BHPH shopper may be deciding whether the down payment and cadence fit. Choose the problem, the store’s actual offer and the next useful page.

Verify the advertised inventory

Check availability, photos, stock number, price and the mobile destination before approving the creative. An ad about one car should take the shopper to that car while available.

Separate cost and outcome

The management fee and dealer-funded media budget are separate. Identify the agreed market, channels, spend and conversion event before a campaign goes live. The examples on the sales page illustrate creative concepts.

Questions dealers ask

Are the sample social ads real dealership campaigns?

They are examples of campaign formats and buying stages. A real launch requires dealer-approved creative, inventory, account access and budget.

Can we advertise in-house financing?

Start with a dealership that offers the terms, verify the actual vehicle and payment details, and have the appropriate reviewer approve the offer and channel before launch.
