Consent management

Contact Permissions

Planned

The planned permissions workspace records which dealership may follow up, through which channel and for what purpose.

Discuss this roadmap ↗View platform screens ↓

Related platform screens

These screens show the tools a dealer can use today alongside this planned product.

Know what the shopper agreed to receive

A buyer who asks for a photo by email has made a specific request. A useful permission record preserves what the buyer asked for and helps staff decide how a later conversation should continue. The cross-channel permissions workspace is planned.

What the proposed cross-channel record contains

A designated store administrator would review the sender, shopper contact, channel, declared purpose, source, capture time and any later withdrawal. The intended record would let the employee responsible for follow-up explain why a particular contact was eligible or suppressed.

Capture the context

The proposed record ties the dealership, contact channel, purpose and wording to the time of the shopper’s choice. That detail matters when different stores in a group serve the same household.

Check before dispatch

A queued follow-up can outlive the shopper’s permission. The planned workflow rechecks the latest choice before sending and routes an interrupted conversation back to staff.

Review a handoff

Have the receptionist and sales manager walk through a one-time reply, an appointment reminder and a promotion. Identify who handles declines and what evidence each person can see.

Questions dealers ask

Will connecting an email or texting provider activate promotions?

A provider connection supplies transport. Campaign activation, audience selection and permission for the intended use are separate setup decisions.

Can I use this page as legal approval for a campaign?

Your dealership’s adviser and messaging provider can review the actual audience, copy and permissions. This page describes a planned dealership workflow.

Run a withdrawal exercise

Start with a shopper who requested an availability reply and later withdrew permission. Decide which planned messages should stop and which employee owns the remaining inquiry.
