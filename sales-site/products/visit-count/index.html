Inside the platform

Select a screenshot to inspect the controls at full size. Screens use demonstration inventory and shopper records.

Measure appointments a salesperson can actually find

The useful point to count is when the dealership has a confirmed appointment. A shopper may open the form twice, ask the concierge to repeat the time or refresh the page while waiting for a response.

Where staff close out a visit

Open Scheduled test drives, find the confirmation, and review the vehicle and time before rescheduling or recording attendance. If staff correct a previously recorded attendance outcome, include the correction note. Use the same confirmation when reconciling the conversion event.

Keep the booking receipt

The booking record carries its confirmation identity and vehicle context. Use it to reconcile a recorded conversion with an appointment staff can open.

Follow the changes

When a shopper calls to move Saturday’s visit to Monday, reschedule the existing appointment. Keep the outcome of that visit distinct from the date of the original request.

Choose the denominator

Use appointments due during a period to discuss attendance. Use qualified inquiries received during a period to discuss booking. A long sales cycle can place those events in different weeks.

Questions dealers ask

What happens if the booking is submitted again?

The booking workflow protects repeated submissions for the same confirmed request. Reconcile counts against confirmation identities when reviewing conversions.

Can we report vehicle sales from attendance alone?

Attendance records show who came in. Confirming a sale requires a matched dealership outcome. Cross-channel revenue attribution is a separate reporting project.

Reconcile a changed appointment

Book a sample visit, move it to another time and inspect the staff record. Identify which date belongs in a booking report and which belongs in an attendance report.
